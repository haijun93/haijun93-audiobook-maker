# [talk_018] Want to be happy? Be grateful

- **문장 수 (Sentence Count)**: 126
- **번역 톤 (Tone)**: 대화체 (Korean Spoken Style - ~습니다/해요체)

| 13 | And we all know people who have lots of misfortune, misfortune that we ourselves would not want to have, and they are deeply happy. | (And we all know people who have lots of misfortune, misfortune that we ourselves would not want to have, and they are deeply happy.) |
